PAIN 02
Storage always full
Giant videos sitting in your gallery until you find the courage to delete them.

Cut, export, rename. Editor work for someone who just wanted to play.
PAIN 05

If no one holds the phone, the play never existed. If someone does, they miss the game.

Fixed cameras and wired arenas don't exist at the local court, the beach or the gym.
« THE SOLUTION

Now the replay goes with you.

You choose how many seconds to save, leave the app running and trigger it when the moment is worth it. KplaWY saves only the configured clip — nothing else.

STEP 01
Choose the seconds
Set how much the replay should save: 10s on Free, up to 50s on Pro.
STEP 02
Position your phone
Tripod, ledge, bag, chair. Frame it once and you're done.
STEP 03
Play like always
The buffer runs on its own in memory. You live the game, not the camera.
STEP 04
Trigger the replay
From the phone, Apple Watch, Wear OS, Bluetooth button or another device.
STEP 05
Only that slice becomes a clip
The last seconds are saved on the device, ready to share.
