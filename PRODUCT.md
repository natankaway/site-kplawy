# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

KplaWY serves amateur and semi-competitive sports players, creators, coaches, and friends who want the best moments from a game without operating a camera during play. They are often on a court, beach, field, gym, or skate spot, using a phone on a tripod and expecting the app to work quickly, offline, and with minimal setup.

## Product Purpose

KplaWY turns a phone into a personal instant replay system. Instead of recording hours and editing later, the app keeps a short continuous buffer and saves only the seconds before the user taps REPLAY. Success means visitors understand the retroactive replay idea immediately, trust that it works on iOS and Android, and download the app.

## Brand Personality

Direct, athletic, and technical. The brand should feel like a night-game replay control room scaled down to a phone: focused, fast, precise, and grounded in real app footage instead of abstract SaaS decoration.

## Anti-references

Do not make KplaWY feel like a generic AI landing page, a decorative card grid, a beige editorial page, or an enterprise SaaS dashboard. Avoid fake device mockups, vague "future of sports" copy, and claims that hide the simple product mechanism.

## Design Principles

- Show the real replay product before explaining the product.
- Make the buffer concept obvious: play first, save afterwards.
- Preserve the dark athletic KplaWY identity while keeping text readable on small phones.
- Treat iOS, Android, watch, Bluetooth, and remote control as production capabilities, not future teasers.
- Keep legal and pricing information consistent with the live app and current site.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for public marketing pages. Maintain strong contrast on the dark palette, ensure all tap targets are at least 44px, avoid clipped sticky CTAs on small Android devices, provide pause control for autoplay media, and honor reduced-motion preferences.
